# CueCueNote

This is the CueCueNote support page. CueCueNote is a music player and listening companion for music production. Compare MAIN and REF tracks, use A-B Loop and audio analysis tools, and save notes while you listen.

[Privacy Policy / プライバシーポリシー / 隐私政策](https://kazunoriri.github.io/DeveloperSite/privacyPolicy/CueCueNote.html)

## English

### CueCueNote Support

CueCueNote is a music player and listening companion for music production. Compare MAIN and REF tracks, use A-B Loop and audio analysis tools, and save notes linked to your listening. You import your own audio files.

For support, email [kazunoriri@gmail.com](mailto:kazunoriri@gmail.com). Please include the app name and your device and iOS version. You do not need to attach audio or personal information.
